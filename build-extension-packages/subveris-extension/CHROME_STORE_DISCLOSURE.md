Subveris is a subscription-usage extension for Premium and Family users. It records supported service domains and time spent there, then sends usage and detected subscription details to the Subveris API. It requests the cookies permission and may inspect cookie names and values on supported services for login signals; it does not intentionally save or transmit the complete cookie jar. Extension storage holds a short-lived session token, user ID, plan status, detected subscriptions, usage signals, and queued data. Optional Gmail scanning requires Google OAuth; it searches matching unread receipt, invoice, or renewal emails and extracts service names and amounts. It does not store passwords, payment-card numbers, keystrokes, complete browsing history, unrelated page contents, or advertising profiles. Production data is sent over HTTPS. Users can log out, revoke Gmail access, clear extension storage, or remove the extension. Contact: help.subveris@gmail.com.
