How to cancel HelloFresh subscription

Whether you want a break, are traveling, or simply want to cut costs, canceling HelloFresh is straightforward as long as you act before the weekly cutoff.

Back to home
1

Step 1: Log in and go to plan settings

Visit the HelloFresh website or open the mobile app and sign in. Then open your name or profile icon, choose Plan Settings or Account Settings, and select the specific meal plan you want to stop.

2

Step 2: Deactivate your account

Scroll to the bottom of the Plan Settings page, look for the status section, and click Cancel Plan or Deactivate My Account. Follow the prompts, skip optional offers, and continue until you reach the final confirmation screen.

3

Step 3: Verify the confirmation email

After the cancellation is processed, HelloFresh will send a confirmation email. Keep it for your records so you have proof that your subscription has been stopped.

Why do so many people cancel HelloFresh?

HelloFresh is often canceled because it is tied to meal plans and delivery frequency, not just a flat monthly habit. People tend to pause or cancel it when their schedule changes, they cook less, or a cheaper option takes over.

  • The regular subscription price becomes too high once promotional discounts expire.
  • It is difficult to use up all the ingredients before they go bad.
  • You are going on vacation or your weekly schedule changes.
  • You want to try competing food boxes or go back to grocery shopping.

What happens after you cancel?

  • The next box may still be scheduled if its account-specific cutoff has passed.
  • Use the deadline shown for your next order and location; delivery cutoffs can vary.
  • After canceling, confirm the plan is inactive and no upcoming delivery remains scheduled.
  • You regain control of your household food budget and recurring expenses.

Frequently asked questions

Why this matters for HelloFresh: HelloFresh is a common subscription to forget because it is tied to weekly deliveries, not a simple monthly charge. Canceling early is usually the best move if you want to avoid the next order or hidden renewal.

Where should I cancel HelloFresh?

Open Plan Settings for the specific meal plan you want to stop. Pausing or skipping one delivery is different from canceling the recurring plan; check every active plan on the account.

How do I confirm HelloFresh is canceled?

Use the next-order deadline displayed in your account for your location. After cancellation, verify the plan is inactive and no upcoming box remains scheduled; keep the confirmation email.

What if I cannot complete cancellation for HelloFresh?

Delivery cutoffs vary by market and order schedule, so do not rely on a universal number of days. If a box is still scheduled, contact HelloFresh before the deadline shown for that order.
