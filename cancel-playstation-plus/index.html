How to cancel PlayStation Plus subscription

Whether you are taking a break from gaming or trying to cut costs, canceling PlayStation Plus and turning off auto renewal is quick and straightforward.

Back to home
1

Step 1: Sign in to account management

Go to playstation.com in your browser, sign in to your Sony account, click your profile avatar at the top-right, and open Account Settings.

2

Step 2: Open subscription management

From the left-hand menu, click Subscription, find your active PlayStation Plus membership, and select Turn Off Recurring Billing.

3

Step 3: Confirm cancellation

Follow the prompts to confirm that you want to stop automatic renewal. Make sure the final screen shows recurring billing is turned off and check your email for confirmation.

Why do so many people cancel PlayStation Plus?

  • Recent price increases have made the annual tiers too expensive.
  • They don't have enough free time to play online multiplayer games.
  • They have cleared their gaming backlog and want to pause their spending.

What happens after cancellation?

  • Your membership remains active until the end of the current billing period.
  • You can keep using the benefits until your current term ends.
  • You can reactivate your membership later if you want to return.

Frequently asked questions

Why this matters for PlayStation Plus: PlayStation Plus is often left active because it is attached to a gaming account and not always revisited after a break. This guide makes sure you stop the auto-renewal in the right account section and keep any remaining time you have already paid for.

Where should I cancel PlayStation Plus?

Use the PlayStation account that purchased the membership and open Account Management > Subscription or Services. Manage recurring billing from that account on the web or console.

How do I confirm PlayStation Plus is canceled?

Check the subscription page for the renewal setting and paid-through date, then keep the confirmation. Turning off auto-renewal generally leaves access until the date shown.

What if I cannot complete cancellation for PlayStation Plus?

If the membership is not visible, check the PlayStation account that received the receipt and whether it is a family-managed account before contacting PlayStation support.
