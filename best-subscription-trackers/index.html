Frequently asked questions

What should I look for in a subscription tracker?

Check whether it records the biller, amount, cadence, and next renewal; whether usage is entered or observed; how cost-per-use and savings are calculated; and whether recommendations are estimates or verified outcomes.

How is a subscription tracker different from a budgeting app?

A subscription-focused tool centers on individual recurring services, renewals, and value per use. A broader budgeting app may be a better fit when the main need is household cash flow, category budgets, or a wider view of accounts.

What makes a subscription optimizer good?

The best tools make it easy to see what you pay, how often you use each service, whether it is underused, and whether it is worth keeping before the next renewal date.

Can I track subscriptions for free?

Yes. Subveris offers a free tier for basic tracking and recurring spend visibility, with more advanced optimization features available on paid plans.
