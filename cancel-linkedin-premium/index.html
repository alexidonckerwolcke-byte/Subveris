How to cancel LinkedIn Premium subscription

This guide shows how to cancel LinkedIn Premium and stop recurring charges for premium career features.

Back to home

Review plan-specific credits before confirming

Before confirming, read the cancellation screen for how your plan handles unused InMail credits and when Premium features end. Credit treatment can depend on the offer and billing arrangement, so rely on the terms shown for your account rather than assuming credits remain usable or disappear immediately.

How to cancel via desktop browser

  1. Click the Me icon (your profile picture) at the top of your LinkedIn homepage.
  2. Select Premium Subscriptions settings from the dropdown menu.
  3. Under Manage Premium Account on the right rail, click Cancel subscription.
  4. Click Continue to cancel and follow the prompts until you hit the confirmation screen.

Note: If you signed up through an iPhone, Apple's privacy policies prevent LinkedIn from cancelling it for you. You must go to your iPhone's Settings → Tap your name → Subscriptions to end it.

Frequently asked questions

Why this matters for LinkedIn Premium: LinkedIn Premium is often paid for with a monthly plan that people forget to revisit after job changes or short-term career periods. This guide focuses on the account settings route so you can stop the renewal without losing access early.

Where should I cancel LinkedIn Premium?

Open LinkedIn's Premium subscription settings. If the purchase appears under Apple subscriptions or another app-store receipt, cancel through that store rather than LinkedIn's website.

How do I confirm LinkedIn Premium is canceled?

Reopen Premium subscription settings and verify the renewal is off and record the access end date. Review any InMail or other plan-specific entitlements shown during cancellation before confirming.

What if I cannot complete cancellation for LinkedIn Premium?

If LinkedIn offers no cancel action, check whether the plan is managed by Apple, an employer, or another organization. Use the original purchase receipt to identify the account owner.

Stop hidden charges before they happen

Compare LinkedIn Premium's recorded use and renewal date with your other career-tool costs before deciding whether to keep it.
