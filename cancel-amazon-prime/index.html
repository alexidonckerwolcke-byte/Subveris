How to cancel Amazon Prime membership

If Prime is no longer worth the monthly fee, this guide walks you through canceling Amazon Prime and stopping future renewals.

Back to home
1

Step 1: Sign in to your Amazon account

Log in to your Amazon account using the email address associated with your Prime membership. Then open Your Account and select Prime Membership to view your current plan, renewal date, and included benefits.

2

Step 2: Manage your membership

Choose Manage Membership and then End Membership or Cancel Membership. Amazon may remind you about the benefits you will lose and ask you to confirm your decision before the cancellation is finalized.

3

Step 3: Confirm the cancellation

Once the process is complete, Amazon will send a confirmation email. You can also revisit the Prime Membership page to verify that automatic renewal has been turned off.

Why do so many people cancel Amazon Prime?

Amazon Prime is easy to keep because it blends convenience and shipping benefits, but many people cancel once the free delivery value drops or they stop using Prime Video. The hard part is often knowing where the renewal is attached in the account.

  • The free trial has ended.
  • You don't shop on Amazon as often anymore.
  • You rarely use Prime Video or other included services.
  • You're looking to reduce monthly expenses.
  • You only signed up for a seasonal promotion or special event.

Because Prime renews automatically, many users continue paying for months without realizing it.

What happens after you cancel?

  • Use the end date and refund option shown by Amazon; available choices can depend on recent use, plan, marketplace, and billing source.
  • Return to Prime Membership and verify that automatic renewal is off or that an end date is displayed.
  • Review which bundled Prime benefits will end on the date shown before completing cancellation.
  • If another provider billed the membership, confirm the change with that provider as well.

Frequently asked questions

Why this matters for Amazon Prime: Amazon Prime is easy to keep subscribed to out of habit, especially if you use the free shipping perk occasionally. The main thing here is checking whether the renewal is attached to your Amazon account, app store account, or a bundled plan.

Where should I cancel Amazon Prime?

Open the Prime Membership or Memberships & Subscriptions page while signed into the Amazon account shown on the charge. If a separate provider bills the membership, use that provider's account.

How do I confirm Amazon Prime is canceled?

Return to the Prime membership page and check that automatic renewal is off and note the end date. Keep Amazon's confirmation email; refund eligibility is shown by Amazon and varies by usage and region.

What if I cannot complete cancellation for Amazon Prime?

If End Membership is unavailable, verify the account and whether the plan is part of a promotion, household, or third-party package. Use the biller shown on the receipt for help.
