Subveris is a subscription-usage extension for authenticated Premium and Family users. It records supported subscription-service domains and time spent on those sites, then sends usage and detected subscription details to the Subveris API for account insights. The extension requests the cookies permission and may inspect cookie names and values on supported services for login/session signals; it does not intentionally save or transmit the complete cookie jar. It stores a short-lived session token, user ID, plan status, detected subscriptions, usage signals, and locally queued data in extension storage. Gmail scanning is optional and requires Google OAuth; the extension searches matching unread receipt/invoice/renewal emails and extracts service names and amounts. It does not store the Subveris password, payment-card numbers, keystrokes, complete browsing history, unrelated page contents, or advertising profiles. Data is transmitted to Subveris over HTTPS in production. Users can log out, revoke Gmail access, clear extension storage, or remove the extension. Contact: help.subveris@gmail.com.
