# Installing Subveris Tracker on Safari (macOS)

Subveris Tracker works on **Safari 15+** on macOS 11 and later.

## Requirements

- **macOS 11+** (Big Sur or later)
- **Safari 15+** (Released September 2021)

## Installation: From Mac App Store (Easiest)

Once Subveris is available on the Mac App Store:

1. **Open App Store** on your Mac
2. **Search for "Subveris Tracker"**
3. **Click "Get"** and authenticate with your Apple ID
4. **Open** the app
5. **Go to Safari → Settings → Extensions**
6. **Enable "Subveris Tracker"**

That's it! You'll receive automatic updates.

## Installation: Direct Load (No App Store)

For macOS Ventura (13) and later, you can load the extension directly:

### Step 1: Enable Developer Mode

1. **Open Safari**
2. Go to **Safari → Settings** (or Preferences on older macOS)
3. Click the **Advanced** tab
4. Check **"Show Develop menu in menu bar"**

### Step 2: Load the Extension

1. **In Safari**, click the **Develop** menu (new menu in top bar)
2. Click **Allow Unsigned Extensions**
3. Go to **Safari → Settings → Extensions** (or Preferences)
4. Click the **+** button at the bottom left
5. Navigate to the Subveris extension folder
6. **Click "Add"**

### Step 3: Grant Permissions

Safari will ask for permission to access websites. Click **"Allow"** to grant full access.

## First Time Setup

After installation:

1. **Open the Subveris app** in Safari (go to subveris.app)
2. **Log in** with your account
3. **Go to Settings → Connected Services**
4. **Click "Connect Gmail Account"** (optional but recommended)
5. **Grant permissions** when Safari asks

✅ Extension is now active and tracking!

## Verify It's Working

1. **Visit a subscription site** (Netflix, Spotify, etc.)
2. **Open the Subveris extension** (toolbar icon)
3. **Look for "Auto Discovery Methods"** section
4. Check your **Dashboard** for newly detected subscriptions

## Troubleshooting

### Extension not appearing in Safari

**Problem**: Extension doesn't show in toolbar  
**Solution**:
1. Go to **Safari → Settings → Extensions**
2. Find "Subveris Tracker" in the list
3. Check that it's enabled (toggle is on)
4. Restart Safari if needed

### Gmail authentication fails

**Problem**: Can't connect Gmail account  
**Solution**:
- Make sure you're logged into Google in Safari
- Check that Safari allows pop-ups for subveris.app:
  1. Click the address bar icon (🔒)
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Ensure pop-ups are allowed
- Try clearing Safari data: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data

### Website tracking not working

**Problem**: Visits to Netflix/Spotify aren't detected  
**Solution**:
1. Verify extension is enabled in Safari Settings → Extensions
2. Check that "Allow on all websites" is enabled for Subveris
3. Visit the subscription site and let it fully load
4. Reload the page if needed
