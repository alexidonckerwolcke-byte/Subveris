How to cancel iCloud subscription

If you cleaned up your storage or want to switch providers, canceling iCloud is simple. This guide shows how to cancel iCloud and keep your data safe.

Back to home
1

Step 1: Open iCloud settings

On your iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, and select iCloud.

2

Step 2: Manage your storage plan

Tap Manage Account Storage or Manage Storage, then choose Change Storage Plan.

3

Step 3: Downgrade to free

Select Downgrade Options, enter your Apple ID password, and choose the 5GB Free plan to cancel your paid subscription. Your plan will switch at the end of the billing cycle.

Why do people cancel iCloud+?

  • They moved their files to a hard drive or Google Drive.
  • They optimized their device storage and no longer need extra space.
  • They want to cut down on recurring monthly fees.

What happens after downgrading?

  • Your plan will switch to the free tier at the end of the current billing cycle.
  • You can still access your existing iCloud data until the change takes effect.
  • You can upgrade again later if you need more storage.

Frequently asked questions

Why this matters for iCloud: iCloud storage is easy to keep paying for without realizing it because the plan is often tied to a device backup and Apple ID. This page helps you confirm whether the storage upgrade is active and how to stop the charge cleanly.

Where should I cancel iCloud?

On Apple devices, open Settings > your Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Account Storage or Manage Storage, then review the storage plan options. Apple manages iCloud+ billing.

How do I confirm iCloud is canceled?

Check that the selected plan and effective date changed. Before downgrading, compare your stored data with the new capacity; backups and syncing can be affected if usage exceeds the plan.

What if I cannot complete cancellation for iCloud?

If the plan change is unavailable, confirm you are using the Apple Account that owns the subscription and retry from its device settings. Do not delete backups as a substitute for changing the plan.
