The Team Owner Trap

If you use Canva Pro within a "Team" setup (even if it's just a small group for a project), only the designated Team Owner can cancel or downgrade the subscription. Regular team members don't have the permissions to do it themselves. Also, make sure to explicitly downgrade to the free plan instead of completely deleting your account; dropping to the free tier safely preserves all your custom designs, it just locks away the premium brand kits and fonts.

How to cancel via desktop browser

  1. Log into your account at Canva.com.
  2. Click the Gear icon (Account settings) in the top right corner.
  3. Select Billing & plans from the left-hand sidebar menu.
  4. Under your plan details, click on the three dots (...) and select Cancel subscription.
  5. Follow the exit steps to confirm your downgrade.

Frequently asked questions

Why this matters for Canva Pro: Canva Pro is often renewed automatically after a design sprint or seasonal project ends. A lot of people assume a browser sign-out cancels it, but the real stop point is the billing section attached to the Canva account.

Where should I cancel Canva Pro?

Open Canva account settings > Billing & plans. For a team plan, the team owner or billing admin may need to make the change; app-store purchases must be managed in that store.

How do I confirm Canva Pro is canceled?

Check Billing & plans for the plan's end date or Free status and save the confirmation. Review which team features, brand assets, and storage change before the downgrade takes effect.

What if I cannot complete cancellation for Canva Pro?

If you cannot see billing controls, verify you are the team owner or billing admin and check the original purchase receipt for an app-store biller.

Stop hidden charges before they happen

Keep Canva Pro's renewal date and recorded use visible with your other recurring tools, and review whether the plan still fits your current projects.
